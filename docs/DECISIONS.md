- 2026-10-30 · D-13: Unified HAND Vertical Datum Formulation. Estimates beyond downstream gauges are flagged 'extrapolated'. Backtest across 14 historical flood events shows 64.3% inside estimated range, MAE of 2.0 hours, and zero status-wrongly-lowered violations · Prevents false precision and unrealistic arrival predictions on regulated reaches · Revert to hydrodynamic routing model if 1D Saint-Venant cross-sections are surveyed.
